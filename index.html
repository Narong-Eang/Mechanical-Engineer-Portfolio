Mechanical design · Coursework

Compressed-air pressure vessel

Problem
Design a vertical Class 2A compressed-air vessel with an 8 m³ internal volume and 862 kPa gauge design pressure.

My role
Designed the vessel for Mechanical Design 2 coursework.

Constraints
Applied AS 1210 requirements and selected material and components for the design.

Process
Calculated shell and head thickness, reinforcement, weld efficiency, corrosion allowance, and hydrostatic test pressure; prepared drawings to AS 1100.

Result
Completed documented design calculations and technical drawings for the vessel.

Tools
SolidWorks, Excel, AS 1210, and AS 1100.
